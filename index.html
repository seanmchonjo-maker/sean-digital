The initiative

Solutions, not just study

Sean Digital is Sean Makoi's endeavour to learn digital skills by creating things that solve real problems. Rather than only studying technology, the work here is shipped, used, and improved. Each project starts from a genuine need and grows from there.

The first project
School Tools

Practical tools for schools

School Tools is the first project under Sean Digital. A growing suite of simple, reliable tools built for the way schools actually work. It comes in variations, each one a focused tool a school can adopt on its own. Two are ready, with more to follow.

School Tools Study

Library & lending

Log every book borrowed and returned with automatic date and time, flag loans that pass their limit, and keep a clear record of what is out and what is back.

Open Study

School Tools Score

Grading & results

Enter every student's marks class by class, compute grades, points and division on the NECTA scales, rank each class, and produce printable result sheets.

Open Score

More variations coming. The suite is built to grow, one focused tool at a time, as schools need them.
